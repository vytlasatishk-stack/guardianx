GuardianX Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

GuardianX is an employee transport management app developed by ManasaTech Innovations (Hyderabad, India). This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Information the app handles

Transport administrators enter employee details such as name, employee ID, mobile number, bus, route and pickup point. Transport staff record boarding and deboarding events, with the shift, bus and time of each scan.

Where the information is stored

All information is stored on the device where the app is used. The app does not send this information to ManasaTech Innovations or to any third-party server.

Permissions

  • Camera: used only to scan QR codes on bus cards and employee ID cards.

Sharing

We do not sell or share employee information. Backups you create are files that you keep yourself.

Security

Passwords are stored as salted hashes, not in plain text. Protect your device with a screen lock, and keep backup files in a safe place.

Children

GuardianX is intended for transport administrators and staff, not for children.
